## Source boundary

The constructor fixes the supplied sucker and a nonzero Ethereum receiver. It rejects unsupported source chains, a peer chain other than Ethereum, an invalid native mapping and missing project/fee ERC-20 tokens. The source project is restricted to V6 project 1 or 3. Construction and sends accept only `ENABLED` or `DEPRECATION_PENDING` sucker state. Token identity comes from the sucker's fixed token registry and project IDs. These are consistency checks against supplied dependencies; independently authenticate them and the destination before funding. [DEPLOYMENT.md](../DEPLOYMENT.md#source-collectors) owns the operational recipe; the [collector audit](../SOURCE_COLLECTOR_AUDIT.md) records the lifecycle admission fix and subsequent validation.

`send()` snapshots the collector's full source-token balance, requires the exact current registry fee, and previews cashout from the source sucker to itself through the current primary native terminal. Its positive minimum is the gross preview less `JBFees.standardFeeAmountFrom(gross)`: a conservative protocol-fee bound, not a swap-price promise. It records the initial outbox count, approves only the source balance, prepares one fixed-beneficiary leaf, clears allowance, requires count to increase by exactly one, submits the native outbox, and requires the sent count to exceed the new leaf index. Every failure reverts the complete transaction.

Paying the registry fee from the same address that receives reserved splits makes an after-prepare balance delta ambiguous when a custom payment callback distributes reserves. A constructor-created fixed fee payer, callable only by its collector and holding no source principal, submits the outbox and returns only its own fee-token delta and newly retained fee to the current caller. Reserved distributions during this call remain in the collector for its next send. Independent source review and root approved this additional bounded address because it enforces the required custody distinction without an assumption about all future fee hooks. Every current and future reserved-split table must name the parent collector, never its child fee payer.

Fee refunds are atomic. The sender rejects preexisting retained credit, claims only its new failed-payment credit directly to the current caller through the existing sucker API, and requires no residual credit. A caller that rejects its own refund aborts its attempt; another caller can retry. No generic rescue or persistent refund ledger is introduced. Unsupported donated assets and preexisting child fee-token donations are not swept or assigned to a caller. Deliberately timed fee-token donations delivered to the child during submission do enter its same-call balance increase; the delta does not authenticate token provenance.
